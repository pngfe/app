Works when the signal does not

Data is expensive and coverage is patchy. PNGFE is built so you download once, then keep going.

  1. Download once

    On Wi-Fi, get your grade's lessons and the map of Papua New Guinea. You can pause and carry on later.

  2. Use it anywhere

    Lessons, quizzes and the map open without signal. Plots and GPS work on the map in the garden or on the road.

  3. Pass it on

    Send a lesson pack or the map to a phone nearby. No data and no internet needed.

Safer school trips

Schools and guardians can follow an excursion on a map, and a child can call for help with one button.

  • Excursions on the mapThe school sets the trip area and who is going.
  • SOS buttonAn alert goes to the school and guardians straight away.
  • Sharing controlsLocation sharing is managed in the app, not left on by default.

The app is free. Download the file to your phone and open it to install.

Version 1.0.0 for Android phones. About the size shown when you download.

How to install

  1. Tap the download button and wait for the file to finish.
  2. Open the file from your notifications or your Downloads folder.
  3. If your phone asks, allow installs from this browser. This is normal for apps that are not from the Play Store.
  4. Open PNGFE and create your account.

Only download PNGFE from this page. Do not install a copy sent by someone you do not know.
